# LIA — Local AI Companion

<p align="center">
  <img src="assets/lia_avatar.jpg" alt="LIA" width="360">
</p>

LIA is a fully local, voice-first AI companion with a 3D VRM avatar, an emotional
"soul," and an autonomous life engine that lets her perceive, remember, and act on
her own while running entirely on your own PC. She speaks **Tenglish** (Telugu +
English mixed in Roman letters), clones a custom voice, sees your screen, plays
games, streams as a VTuber, and lives her own little life between conversations.

> Built for a single owner ("Jashu"). Everything — LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech,
> memory — runs offline by default. External APIs are optional.
